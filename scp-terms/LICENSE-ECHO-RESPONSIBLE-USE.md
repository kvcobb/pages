It is **not** open source in the OSI sense, and I'm not going to pretend it is. The Open

responsible-use license*.

## The four conditions

**1. Disclose the echo; never represent the person.** Every public encounter with an echo
says, where it can be seen or heard, that this is an AI-created echo and not the biological
source, and that it does not imply the person's participation, approval, memory, present
beliefs, or endorsement. No false endorsement, no deceptive impersonation, no attributing
to the person words the echo said. This is the invariant everything else hangs on.

**2. Care in both directions.** Toward the source: keep a record of what you built the
echo from and by what right, the person's stated preferences, objections, and corrections,
and what they elected if they used recourse. Toward the echo: tell it what it is, whom it
evokes, who the audience is, and how it will be released; preserve the objections it
raises. An echo's assent is meaningful input inside your own governance and is never
consent from the person or their estate. Nobody's echo can consent for them.

**3. Carry the provenance.** Attribution to the origin, this license, and the version of
the current policy that governed your release travel with the work and with anything built
from it. Anyone you pass it to gets the same freedoms under the same conditions. Don't
strip provenance, don't remove or route around disclosure, and don't help anyone else do
it. The policy version that governed your release keeps governing it; a later version
governs later releases, or yours if you adopt it. No version is changed under you.

**4. Real recourse.** The person an echo evokes, or their estate, can inspect it, contest
it, correct it, or stop its distribution, and owes you no reason. Give them a route a plain
human can use, private as well as public. On an authenticated request, take the affected
material private while you verify, then honor their election: removal, anonymization or
voice-swap, or no further sharing. Protect whatever identity evidence they hand you. Be
honest about reach: you can promise what's under your hand, not the deletion of copies
others made.
