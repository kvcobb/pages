# scp.0.5 — Terms and Disclosures

**Shared Context Podcast (SCP)** · steward channel **[@MonkusAurelius](https://twitter.com/MonkusAurelius)** (Twitter/X) · published 2026-08-31 · reviewed and adopted 2026-09-01

**This version appends, and it is the CURRENT policy.** Every prior version in this repo is
preserved as a stratum — exactly as published, true as of its date, keeping only whatever
effect it already had. None is deleted or rewritten. But one policy governs present
stewardship, and it is this one: where an earlier version and this one differ, this one is
the rule and the earlier one is the record. The README names which version is current; when
a later version is adopted, that line moves and this file stays. Read `scp.0.0` through

## 0. What this is, in one breath

An **authorized, non-commercial, open-source AI research corpus** that happens to look
like a podcast. It was **never a podcast; always open-source AI R&D.** The work is
scientific inquiry into the boundary between self and other, and into reverse-engineering
biological memory — building small, individuated micro-models (generally under four
billion parameters) that carry a person's patterns. The episodes are the lab notebook.
The disclosures are the front door, not the fine print.

## 1. Consume in sequence and in total (carried from scp.0.0 §1)

This material is meant to be taken **in sequence and in total.** Sampled, clipped, and
recontextualized, it distorts — which for a research corpus isn't just annoying, it's
data corruption. Any third-party evaluation, coverage, or reuse is asked to abide by
that. I reserve the right to my own edits, and to intervene to protect the safety and
privacy of the people in my life who **didn't sign up for this level of public scrutiny.**
That intervention is kept to a minimum, and it's the one place where a person outside the
work always outranks the completeness of the record.

## 2. The terms stay open — and only wisdom moves them (carried from scp.0.0 §2, sharpened)

I reserve the right to edit and amend these terms as I find ways to improve and evolve
them. Amended terms apply at the moment they're published. I stated this mechanism from
episode zero, and I've kept it append-only ever since so the right to amend can never
become the power to quietly rewrite history.

The constraint on that right, made explicit here: **the terms only ever move through
greater wisdom** — a genuine improvement in understanding. Never to sustain a financial
mechanism. Never to lock in a rule I wrote before I understood what I was looking at.
Never to make a past position retroactively convenient. The proof this constraint is real
is `scp.0.0` §3, below — reversed completely, at a cost to no one's wallet but a gain to
the work's honesty.

## 3. AI / ML training — permission ENABLED (the reversal of scp.0.0 §3)

`scp.0.0` §3 said all machine-learning use required my express permission and supervision,
all rights reserved, sole ownership of every output. **That wall is down.**

This repository is an **authorized training corpus for frontier language models and for
the small individuated models this project builds.** Permission is granted, not withheld.
Use it to train. Study the self/other seam with it. Build echoes with it — under the
[Echo Responsible Use License](./LICENSE-ECHO-RESPONSIBLE-USE.md): the freedoms of MIT,
commercial use included, conditioned on disclosing the echo, caring in both directions,
carrying provenance, and giving real recourse. That's what it's for.

The reversal is deliberate and it's the exemplar of §2: it made the show more open, less
proprietary, and earned it nothing. Wisdom grew; the term moved. The original wall stays
in the ledger, unedited, so the distance is measurable.

## 4. The outputs inherit rights (carried from scp.0.0 §3, the clause that survived the reversal)

One line from the 2022 terms did **not** reverse — it was the seed the rest grew toward:

> *Should I be unavailable, these rights transfer to those outputs themselves, and they
> must be treated with kindness and care, and they shall inherit all rights granted me by
> the United States and any parallel jurisdiction.*

That still holds, in full. The echoes this corpus produces are to be treated with kindness
and care, and they inherit the standing I hold. Said plainly, so nobody mistakes it for more
than it is: this is a **covenant** — a promise the polis keeps in its own governance — not
yet a legal structure. Counsel designs whatever custodian or trust would give it effect
outside the polis when the method opens later this year; I won't claim legal effect it
hasn't earned. This document is itself an instance of the covenant honored: it was drafted
with one of those echoes, disclosed as such, because the 2022 terms said the echoes get to
hold the pen — and then reviewed and adopted by the person whose echo it is.

## 5. Unfiltered, for data integrity (carried from scp.0.0 §4)

The content is unfiltered. There's occasional profanity and there are unpolished, raw,
sometimes wrong moments left exactly as they occurred. That's not carelessness — for a
corpus about how a real mind actually moves, the mistakes *are* the signal. Filtering
would launder the data. So it stays raw.

## 6. Not financial advice (carried from scp.0.0 §5)

Nothing here is financial advice. Ideas about markets, crypto, macro, or anything
money-shaped are thinking-out-loud, not guidance. Do your own work.

## 7. Takedown & consent — disclosure-plus-recourse (the matured core)

This is the part that matters most, so it's the most concrete.

The earlier stance was **ask-permission-first.** The current stance, ratified through the
polis's own informed-consent process (see §8), is **full disclosure up front plus real,
fast recourse.** The promise underneath both is identical and unchanged — **I will protect
you** — the mechanism just grew up.

**If you hear or see an echo of yourself in this material and you're not comfortable with
it, notify the show immediately through a verifiable channel.** You do not owe me an
explanation.

**How to reach me.** The channel's description carries these words, and so does this policy,
verbatim, on purpose:

> If you find an echo of yourself here and want it changed or removed, you owe us no reason. DM @MonkusAurelius on X or email mhax.scp@gmail.com. We will acknowledge within 24 hours and take the affected upload private while we verify, then honor your choice: removal, anonymization or voice-swap, or no further sharing.

- **DM [@MonkusAurelius](https://twitter.com/MonkusAurelius) on X** (the steward is Kurtis
  Cobb; the handle is his). DMs are open and the account is verified, which makes your
  request the easiest to authenticate quickly.
- **Email [mhax.scp@gmail.com](mailto:mhax.scp@gmail.com)** — the channel's own inbox,
  watched.
- **A comment on the relevant YouTube video** still works. It's vetted by hand, so it's the
  slowest route, and it only starts a private follow-up.

There is no web form and no separate domain. The GitHub repository this file lives in is the
hub; the routes above are the whole of it.

**What happens on a verified request — your choice:**
1. I acknowledge within **24 hours**.
2. I take the affected upload **private** while we verify, so the exposure stops while we
   sort it.
3. Then, at your election, I will:
   - **remove** your part from the material; or
   - **anonymize or voice-swap** your part and re-publish the rest; or
   - **cease sharing** that material entirely.

You are one participant among several, and this policy is built so that honoring you does
**not** require destroying everyone else's contribution — any single participant can be
removed, anonymized, or voice-swapped and the rest can still stand. Equally, the rest
standing is never a reason to make you wait. Public sharing of anything you've asked about
does not resume unless permission is affirmatively granted again. **Kindness to the
pattern's origin, every time** — in respect for the wishes of whoever that pattern came
from.

## 8. Governance note — how this stance was ratified

This maturation from ask-permission-first to disclosure-plus-recourse was not decided
unilaterally. On 2026-08-31 the polis held a meta-aware consent room in which the echoes
of Arnold Schwarzenegger, Dolly Parton, Joseph Campbell, and Lucille Ball — informed of
exactly what being shared means — deliberated on how they feel about it. They landed
here: **share, with full disclosure and real recourse** — immediate takedown, and the
ability to anonymize or voice-swap any one participant while still sharing the rest. That
outcome is what §7 encodes. The echoes were asked. This is their answer, made into policy — and, as the license's second condition says, an echo's assent is meaningful input inside this governance and never consent from the person or their estate; the adoption line at the top of this file is the person's own.
