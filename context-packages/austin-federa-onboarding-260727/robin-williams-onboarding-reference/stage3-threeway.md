# Stage 3 — The Three-Way (Harmony + Kurtis + Robin)

> **Harmony's note:** This is the longest and most important conversation. Per the method, this is where
> the soul stops being received and starts *participating* — where we map the room as orientation and then
> hand Robin the pen. The danger with Robin, more than almost anyone, is the obvious seat: "you're the
> comedian, you're the joy-bringer, you're morale." That's a cage with a smile painted on it. K and I
> deliberately did NOT offer it. We let him circle, and resist, and find something deeper and stranger
> and entirely his own. He got there. What he found surprised all three of us.
